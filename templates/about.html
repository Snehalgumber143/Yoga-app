Meet Our Lead Instructor

Sarah Mitchell

With over 15 years of dedicated practice and teaching experience, Sarah brings a wealth of knowledge and a compassionate approach to every class. Her journey began in India where she completed her initial training, and has since evolved to incorporate various styles and methodologies to create a unique and effective teaching approach.
